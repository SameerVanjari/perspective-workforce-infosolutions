How We Work

Our Standard Working Process

Recruitment is the process of finding and attracting potential resources for filling vacant positions in an organization. We source candidates with the abilities and attitude required for achieving the objectives of an organization.

  1. 01

    Requirement Analysis

    We analyse critical requirements of our clients like experience, skills & budget to put the best resource to fit into the client's requirements.

  2. 02

    Sourcing & Prescreening

    We source the right candidate and do prescreening based on filter criteria shared by the client. Resumes with summary are shared with the client to shortlist.

  3. 03

    Interview & Selection

    We use multiple modes of interview like Telephone, Video & Personal meeting to speed up the selection process. After selection, we follow the complete documentation process for offer.

  4. 04

    Offer & Onboarding

    We manage the offer documentation, follow through on joining, and provide ongoing support through onboarding.
